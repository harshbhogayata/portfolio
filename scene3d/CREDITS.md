# The scene: sources and licence

The scene is built in code: the lake and its banks, the landmarks, the town, the boats. Two things in it come from photographs.

**The surfaces of the landmarks.** Each landmark is modelled in code to the measure of the real building; the stone, arcades and carving on its walls, towers and domes are pieces cut from a photograph of it (`tiles-data.js`).

**The statue.** Its outline and relief (`statue-data.js`) were made by a script from a photograph, and its base wears stone cut from the same one.

The photographs were cropped to parts of each building, straightened where a tower or dome stood against the sky, and lit as at night in the scene. Because they are adapted from share-alike photographs, `tiles-data.js` and `statue-data.js` are shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This covers those two files only.
