# The scene: how it is made

Everything in the scene is built in code: the lake and its banks, the landmarks, the statue, the town, the boats. No photograph is part of it, and nothing in it was made from one by a script.

**The landmarks.** Laxmi Vilas Palace, Kirti Mandir, Nyay Mandir, Mandvi Gate, the Arts Faculty of MS University and the Baroda Museum are each modelled on all four sides and over their roofs: walls with openings cut through them, arcades, balconies, cornices, domes, tiled roofs. Their stone, brick and tile are drawn in code (`mason.js`).

**The statue.** Sarveshwar Mahadev is modelled in the round (`statue.js`): the figure, the cobra, the beads, the trident with its cloth and drum, the tiger skin, the fluted mass behind his legs, on the grey granite pedestal he has stood on since he was gilded in 2023.

**What they were measured from.** Proportions and details were taken by eye and by measurement from published photographs of the real buildings and of the statue, among them photographs on Wikimedia Commons by Sharadsy, Snehrashmi, Christian Köhler, Cutekutty_rk and Rajeswaran Sivasankar. The photographs were looked at, not used: none of their pixels are in the scene.

An earlier version of the scene did wear pieces of those Commons photographs on its walls, and carried the share-alike condition that came with them. Those files (`tiles-data.js`, `statue-data.js`) are gone.
