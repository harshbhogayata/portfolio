# The scene: sources and licence

Everything in the scene is built in code from simple solids: the lake and its banks, the palace and the other landmarks, the town, the boats.

The one exception is the statue. Its outline and relief (`statue-data.js`) were made by a script from this photograph:

| Used for | Photograph on Wikimedia Commons | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sarveshwar Mahadev statue | [Shiva Statue @ Sur Sagar Lake, Vadodara.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shiva_Statue_@_Sur_Sagar_Lake,_Vadodara.JPG) | Rajeswaran Sivasankar | CC BY-SA 3.0 |

The photograph was cut to the figure; its outline, a rounded height and its light and shade are kept, and the shade is turned to gold in the scene. Because it is adapted from a share-alike photograph, `statue-data.js` is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This covers that file only.

Replacing it with a model or photograph of your own removes this condition.
