# The skyline: sources and licence

The drawings in this folder are Vadodara's landmarks, converted from photographs. Each `.txt` holds the characters (a day drawing, a night drawing and a mask); each `.png` holds the building's outline.

## What was changed

Each photograph was cropped to the building, its sky and surroundings were removed, and its tones were stretched. The result was converted to characters with the image-to-ASCII tool at [ascii-magic.com](https://www.ascii-magic.com/tools/image-to-ascii) (standard ramp, ` .:-=+*#%@`), once with dark as dense for the day drawing and once with bright as dense for the night drawing. The outline is the same cut-out, saved as a shape.
